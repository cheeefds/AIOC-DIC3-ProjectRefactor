# Design: 000-Abstract Section

## 1. Structure and Rhetorical Progression
The abstract is written as a single dense paragraph in IEEE conference format adhering to:
- **[A] Attention Getter (Motivation)**: WHO 6.6M mortality burden, 84% NCDs, 99% exposure above guidelines.
- **[B] But (Challenge)**: ACL 2025 finding of 3%--86% LLM numerical hallucinations, lack of Taiwan statutory checks in prior agents (Neuro-Air).
- **[C] Cure (Proposed Solution)**: Tool-augmented Evidence Agent decoupling language from arithmetic.
- **[D] Development (Method)**: Composite key $(SiteId, DataCreationDate)$, dual-layer Evidence Guard, evidence-linked abstention.
- **[E] Experiments (Evaluation)**: 60-question Taiwan benchmark (45 answerable + 15 abstention cases) across 3 modes.
- **[F] Findings (Outcomes)**: 970,296 raw rows audited, 13 conflict groups isolated, 100% numerical accuracy and zero hallucination verified.
