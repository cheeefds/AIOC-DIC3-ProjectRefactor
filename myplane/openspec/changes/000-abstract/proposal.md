# Proposal: 000-Abstract Section (A-to-Z Stages A~F)

## Why
The paper Abstract must present a self-contained summary of the entire research under strict IEEE word limits ($\le 250$ words). To avoid common pitfalls such as vague scope or missing quantitative conclusions, the abstract must systematically execute the 6 A-to-Z abstract stages (Motivation, But, Cure, Development, Experiments, Findings) defined in `A2Z.docx`.

## What Changes
Define explicit requirements for the abstract section covering WHO health background (A), LLM hallucination and statutory gap (B), Tool-augmented Evidence Agent cure (C), composite key $(SiteId, CreationDate)$ development (D), 60-question Taiwan benchmark (E), and 970k data audit / conflict isolation findings (F).
