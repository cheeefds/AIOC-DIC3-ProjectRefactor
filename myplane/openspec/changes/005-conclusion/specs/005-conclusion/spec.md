# Spec Delta: 005-Conclusion

## Purpose
Specifies the four-element synthesis requirements for the Conclusion section following A-to-Z Stages W~Z.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Four-Part Concluding Synthesis
The Conclusion section SHALL synthesize the research across the 4 closing dimensions: What proposed (W), eXcel domain strengths (X), Yields tangible deliverables (Y), and Zen future roadmap (Z).

#### Scenario: Verify conclusion content
- **WHEN** Section V is read
- **THEN** it articulates the decoupled architecture (W), auditable provenance (X), open-source artifacts (Y), and future real-time IoT extensions (Z)
